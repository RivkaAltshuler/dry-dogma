About this demonstration and its scope

Purpose

This animation is a simplified illustration to help explain constructive assignment.

Scope and open question

Covering all the correct behavior in this scenario requires additional assignments.

In this example, bidirectional constraints may seem like the right choice from the start. This animation does not explain why the language does not use them; its purpose is only to illustrate and teach constructive assignment.
